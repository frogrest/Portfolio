### Design Specificity Verdict
**Authored for Gian Carlo Noriega (High Specificity)**

The portfolio avoids generic developer templates. The visual language authentically unites full-stack software development with video editing, VFX, and Unreal Engine interactive game prototyping. The obsidian canvas and warm brass palette immediately convey the atmosphere of a creative mastering suite.

---

### What's Working
1. **Rapid Recruiter Scanability**: The profile snapshot bento card and categorized skills matrix allow hiring managers to evaluate core competencies in under 15 seconds.
2. **Dual-Domain Credibility**: Video post-production and software engineering are presented with equal technical rigor and visual polish.
3. **Flawless Responsive Physics**: Fluid layouts across 375px mobile and 1280px desktop with 100% WCAG 2.5.5 touch target compliance.

---

### Priority Issues & Opportunities
- **[P2] Card Interactivity**: Project cards feel static on hover. Adding a subtle 3D perspective tilt and radial cursor spotlight glow will provide tangible physical feedback.
- **[P2] Contact Copy Utility**: Adding one-click copy-to-clipboard for email and phone with an animated toast notification will streamline recruiter outreach.
- **[P3] Hero Atmosphere**: An ambient particle constellation canvas in the Hero background will visually reinforce the "Creative Technologist" identity.
